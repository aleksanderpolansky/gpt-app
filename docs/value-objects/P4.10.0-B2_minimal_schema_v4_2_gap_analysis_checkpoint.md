## What should stay untouched now

- Do not add owner_user_id to value_objects now.
- Do not rename category_id to contextual_category_id.
- Do not replace category_table/category_id with a narrower FK too early.
- Do not introduce typed relation edge tables.
- Do not introduce tags/entity_tags.
- Do not redesign commercial core.
- Do not remove legacy docs just because older assumptions exist.
