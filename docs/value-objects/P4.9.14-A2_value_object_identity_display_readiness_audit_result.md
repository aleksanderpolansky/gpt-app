## Decision

Reason:

- hierarchy write readiness from this audit means “can be reviewed”, not “safe to auto-link”;
- title identity alone is not enough for structural hierarchy;
- parent/child hierarchy must stay semantically approved;
- current available candidates do not yet include clear structural parents like Learning, Health, German language, Knee, Massage, Service, or Product category nodes.
