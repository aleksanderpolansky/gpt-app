3. Why business / sales is the next domain

Family / childcare benchmark proved that a phrase can require deeper semantic layers beyond visible nouns.

Example from C8-F logic:
- "учил математику с ребёнком" is not only math + child + learning.
- It also requires role/care/purpose meanings such as childcare, parental-care, helping-child-learn.
- Ambiguous phrases must not overclaim active teaching.

Business / sales has the same structural problem.

Example:
"писал коммерческое предложение клиенту"

This is not only:
- writing
- document
- client

It also includes:
- B2B sales
- work responsibility
- customer responsibility
- income generation / revenue generation
- business development
- commercial intent

But the system must not overclaim:
- closed deal
- payment received
- contract signed
- meeting happened
- customer support case
unless explicit evidence exists.

4. Core semantic layers for business/sales benchmark

Layer: action
Purpose: what the user did.
Examples: write, send, call, negotiate, update, prepare, research, analyze, discuss, learn

Layer: object / domain
Purpose: what the action was about.
Examples: proposal, offer, CRM, lead list, sales script, client objection, pricing, partnership terms, B2B sales

Layer: participant
Purpose: who was involved or targeted.
Examples: client, potential-client, lead, partner, colleague, friend, prospect

Layer: relationship_context
Purpose: social/business context.
Examples: work, business, B2B, marketplace, partnership, sales-process

Layer: role
Purpose: in which role the user acted.
Examples: seller, sales-manager, consultant, business-developer, negotiator, account-manager, learner

Layer: duty / responsibility
Purpose: what responsibility the activity served.
Examples: work-responsibility, customer-responsibility, sales-responsibility, partner-responsibility, pipeline-responsibility

Layer: purpose / activity_meaning
Purpose: why the activity was performed.
Examples: income-generation, revenue-generation, client-acquisition, lead-nurturing, deal-preparation, objection-handling, relationship-management, professional-development

Layer: metric
Purpose: how the activity is measured.
Examples: duration-minutes, number-of-calls, number-of-leads, number-of-messages, amount-money

Layer: ambiguity / risk
Purpose: prevent overclaiming.
Examples: possible-learning-only, possible-informal-conversation, possible-admin-only, possible-sales-context, not-confirmed-client-work

7. Minimum expected benchmark behavior

The future C8-G patch/proof should show:

- clear sales work is classified as sales / business / responsibility / purpose
- preparatory learning is classified as preparation/professional-development, not actual sales
- informal sales-topic conversation is not overclassified as work responsibility
- lead/prospect/potential-client are not collapsed into existing client
- support after purchase is not overclassified as new sales
- negotiation can be partner/business responsibility without client-sale overclaim
- income/revenue generation can be possible or purpose only where evidence supports it

8. Forbidden overclaim list for the whole group

The extractor/classifier must not infer the following unless explicit evidence exists:

- closed-deal
- contract-signed
- payment-received
- invoice-paid
- successful-sale
- discount-given
- meeting-held
- proposal-sent
- client-acquired
- existing-client
- customer-responsibility
- work-responsibility
- income-generation

Important nuance:
Some of these can be possible meanings or secondary candidates in specific cases, but must not be required/core if the phrase does not support them.

9. Candidate confidence policy for future C8-G-B/C/D

Suggested confidence bands:

High confidence / required:
- explicit client + proposal
- explicit B2B lead + discovery call
- explicit follow-up to client after meeting
- explicit price objections
- explicit CRM after lead call
- explicit outbound potential-client list

Medium confidence / possible:
- sales-related reading
- informal sales discussion
- business partner negotiation where sales context is unclear
- income-generation where commercial intent exists but outcome is unknown

Low confidence / do not canonicalize automatically:
- sales word appears only as topic
- friend/family discussion without work context
- learning content without client/work action
- support-only context that may not be sales
