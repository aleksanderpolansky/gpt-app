## 1. Purpose

Find all runtime/schema references related to country-to-currency mapping and currency fields after C1/C2.

Business rule to preserve:

- one organization has exactly one country;
- organization.default_currency is derived from organization.country_code;
- organization currency must not be arbitrary manual input;
- purchase confirmation currency should derive from organization.default_currency;
- spent certificate points are burned, not transferred to seller.
