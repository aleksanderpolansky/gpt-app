Status: INSPECTION ONLY

## 1. Purpose

Inspect how purchase confirmation currency is currently read/written before planning synchronization between purchase_currency and currency.

Business rules to preserve:

- purchase confirmation is organization-level external purchase confirmation;
- purchase confirmation is not cart/order/item flow;
- purchase confirmation currency should derive from organization.default_currency;
- purchase_confirmations.purchase_currency is current active runtime field;
- purchase_confirmations.currency is duplicate/additive and must not become separate business source;
- points spent on certificates are burned, not transferred to seller.
