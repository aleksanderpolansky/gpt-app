## 8. Business rules preserved

- One organization has exactly one country.
- Organization currency is derived from organization.country_code.
- organization.default_currency remains stored organization currency.
- Purchase confirmation currency should derive from organization.default_currency.
- Points spent on certificates are burned, not transferred to seller.
- Seller receives only the money part of certificate payment.
