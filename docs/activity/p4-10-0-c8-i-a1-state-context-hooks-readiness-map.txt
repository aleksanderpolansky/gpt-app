Core rule:
A hook is not a state fact.
A hook is not ontology truth.
A hook is not a medical, financial, emotional or social claim.
A hook is only a candidate signal that may later be processed by the C8-I-A2 fact/signal contract.

Hook lifecycle:
1. Activity/event/free text contains semantic material.
2. Category derivation may produce category candidates and semantic hooks.
3. Hooks remain inactive/candidate-level until validated by fact/signal rules.
4. C8-I-A2 decides whether a hook is:
   - direct measurement
   - user self-report
   - semantic hook
   - inferred state
   - risk flag
   - forbidden overclaim
5. Only after later contracts/migrations may state facts/deltas/snapshots be persisted.

Universal hook fields expected later:
- hookKey
- hookGroup
- sourceTextFragment or evidence summary
- sourceType
- confidence
- privacyLevel

- possibleStateDimensions

- correctionAllowed

============================================================
1. Family / care / social role hooks
============================================================

Examples of input:
- I helped my child with math.
- I cooked dinner for the family.
- I drove my daughter to school.
- I had a difficult talk with my partner.
- I took care of my mother.
- I had to interrupt work because of a family obligation.

Candidate hooks:
- family_context
- childcare
- caregiving
- parental_care
- family_obligation
- household_responsibility
- social_role_parent
- social_role_partner
- social_role_caregiver
- family_support_activity
- family_conflict_signal
- interrupted_by_family_context

Possible future state dimensions:
- family_care_load
- social_obligation_load
- recovery_constraint
- attention_fragmentation
- time_pressure
- emotional_load

Allowed interpretation:
- This activity may indicate family/care involvement.
- This activity may consume time, attention or recovery resources.
- This activity may be relevant to Next Best Action scheduling.

Forbidden overclaims:
- Do not infer family relationship quality.
- Do not infer child wellbeing.
- Do not infer psychological diagnosis.
- Do not infer conflict severity without explicit user report.
- Do not infer moral evaluation of the user.

Required evidence for future fact:
- user self-report
- repeated activity pattern
- explicit role/relationship metadata
- optional calendar/time evidence
- user correction/confirmation

C8-I-A2 handling:
semantic_hook by default.
Can become user_self_report only if user explicitly says "I feel", "I am responsible for", "I must", "I am exhausted because of".

============================================================
10. Risk / forbidden overclaim hooks
============================================================

Risk triggers:
- medical terms without evidence
- hormone claims without measurement
- psychological diagnosis terms
- financial/legal conclusions
- social ranking or social scoring
- sensitive identity or protected attributes
- exact location
- family/child wellbeing claims
- workplace legal conclusions
- AI certainty language

Candidate hooks:
- medical_overclaim_risk
- hormone_overclaim_risk
- diagnosis_overclaim_risk
- financial_overclaim_risk
- legal_overclaim_risk
- privacy_sensitive_context
- location_sensitive_context
- social_rating_risk
- child_sensitive_context
- workplace_sensitive_context

Possible future state dimensions:
- not a normal state dimension by default
- used as validation and suppression layer
- may influence privacy/risk handling

Allowed interpretation:
- Output must be softened or blocked.
- The system should ask for confirmation/evidence when needed.
- The system should avoid converting ambiguous text into factual claims.

Forbidden overclaims:
- Any unsupported diagnosis.
- Any unsupported hormone state.
- Any unsupported legal/tax/financial certainty.
- Any social score.
- Any hidden inference about sensitive personal attributes.

Required evidence for future fact:
- explicit user statement
- explicit document/evidence
- professional source only when appropriate
- user permission
- privacy/sensitivity handling

C8-I-A2 handling:
forbidden_overclaim or risk_flag.
