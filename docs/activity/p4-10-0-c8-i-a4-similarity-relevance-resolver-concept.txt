============================================================
1. Definitions
============================================================

Similarity:
Similarity means that two Value Objects, activities, categories or contexts share semantic structure.
Similarity is based on overlap, distance or closeness between category signatures, labels, descriptions, directions or observed patterns.

Relevance:
Relevance means that one object, state, activity or context matters for another object or decision.
Relevance may exist even when two objects are not semantically similar.

Influence:
Influence means that there is a hypothesized or evidenced directional effect.
Influence is stronger than relevance and requires stricter evidence.

Resolver:
The resolver is a future layer that separates and combines similarity edges, relevance edges, influence rules and current constraints for Next Best Action.

============================================================
2. Non-negotiable separation
============================================================
- Two language-learning activities may be similar because they share language_learning, grammar, practice and study categories.
- Sleep is relevant to language learning because it may affect focus and recovery, but sleep is not semantically similar to grammar practice.
- Financial pressure is relevant to career and B2B sales activity, but it is not similar to a sales call.
- Family care load is relevant to time pressure and recovery need, but it is not similar to coding or marketing work.
- Walking may be relevant to recovery, energy or physical_load, but it is not similar to writing a business proposal.
- A CRM follow-up and a job interview preparation may be partially similar through career/business context, but their relevance depends on current goals.

Rule:
Similarity edge and relevance edge must be modeled separately in future schema and logic.

3. Similarity edge concept
============================================================

Future candidate table:
value_object_similarity_edges

Purpose:
Represent semantic closeness between two Value Objects or between derived semantic signatures.

Possible future fields:
- source_value_object_id
- target_value_object_id
- similarity_score
- similarity_basis
- shared_category_keys
- shared_dimension_keys
- signature_version
- confidence
- evidence_summary
- calculated_at
- expires_at or refresh_policy
- correction_allowed

Possible similarity_basis values:
- semantic_category_overlap
- shared_direction
- shared_activity_pattern
- shared_actor_context
- shared_offer_or_commercial_context
- shared_learning_context
- shared_physical_context
- manual_user_link

Allowed use:

- Suggest reusable categories.
- Suggest consolidation or hierarchy candidates.

Forbidden use:

- Do not treat similarity as relevance.
- Do not infer state facts from similarity alone.
- Do not create mandatory Value Object subtypes from similarity clusters.
- Do not make medical, financial, emotional or legal claims from similarity.

============================================================
5. State relevance rules concept
============================================================

Future candidate table:
state_relevance_rules

Purpose:
Define conservative rules that connect state dimensions to recommendation behavior.

Example rules:
- fatigue high may decrease priority of intense cognitive or physical tasks.
- recovery_need high may increase priority of low-load or recovery actions.
- focus low may decrease priority of deep analytical work.
- time_pressure high may increase priority of micro-actions.
- environment_fit poor may suppress tasks requiring computer or quiet context.
- financial_pressure high may increase priority of business_development_effort or career actions.
- family_care_load high may constrain available time and increase need for flexible tasks.
- learning_progress_signal weak may suggest review or active recall, not automatic level claim.
- goal_balance under_attended may raise a direction in weakest-direction list.

Rule requirements:
- Every rule must have allowed interpretation.
- Every rule must have forbidden overclaims.
- Every rule must be explainable to the user.
- Every rule must be correctable or suppressible by user feedback.
- Every rule must respect privacy and sensitivity level.
- Rules must not create medical, legal, financial or social certainty.

============================================================
6. Similarity scoring concept
============================================================

Similarity score may later be calculated from:
- weighted category overlap
- shared category depth
- shared direction
- shared activity type
- shared actor role
- shared object hierarchy
- shared metric pattern
- user-confirmed similarity
- negative evidence when user rejects similarity

Initial scoring idea:
- category overlap can be the first weak score.
- deep shared categories may weigh more than generic categories.
- user-confirmed similarity should weigh more than automatic similarity.
- rejected similarity should reduce or block repeated suggestions.
- similarity score should be provisional until user feedback or stable pattern confirms it.

Forbidden scoring behavior:
- No hidden social scoring.
- No sensitive-attribute similarity unless explicitly permitted and safe.
- No medical similarity claim.
- No deterministic personality classification.
- No automatic hierarchy merge without user confirmation.

============================================================
7. Relevance scoring concept
============================================================

Relevance score may later be calculated from:
- current selected direction
- weakest or under-attended direction
- current MVP state dimensions
- time window
- environment fit
- goal priority
- known obligations
- risk flags
- user feedback
- recency
- repeated patterns
- explicit user instruction

Initial scoring idea:
- selected user direction is the strongest relevance modifier.
- risk flags can suppress recommendations even if relevance is high.
- fatigue/recovery can lower intensity recommendations.
- time_pressure can shift from deep action to micro-action.
- financial_pressure can raise business/career actions but cannot claim income certainty.
- goal_balance can raise under-attended directions, but user must choose direction.

Forbidden relevance behavior:
- Do not choose automatically without user direction selection in NBA v0.
- Do not use hidden sensitive inferences.
- Do not rank the person morally.
- Do not imply professional medical/legal/financial advice.
- Do not use relevance score as a social score.

============================================================
8. Resolver pipeline concept
============================================================

Future resolver pipeline:
- Input: activity/event/category bundle/value object candidates/state signals.
- Build or read semantic signatures.
- Compute similarity candidates separately.
- Compute relevance candidates separately.
- Apply state relevance rules.
- Apply privacy and sensitivity gates.
- Apply risk and forbidden_overclaim suppression.
- Generate explainable candidate links.
- Ask user for confirmation or correction where needed.
- Use safe resolver output in NBA ranking only after user direction selection.

Resolver output must include:
- similarity candidates
- relevance candidates
- suppressed candidates
- risk flags
- explanation summary
- confidence
- privacy level
- correction options

============================================================
9. Next Best Action use
============================================================

NBA v0 must use resolver output conservatively.

NBA v0 process remains:
- Detect weak or under-attended directions.
- Show direction list from weakest to strongest.
- Ask user which direction they want to work on now.
- After user choice, create action candidates.
- Use relevance to filter and rank candidates.
- Use similarity to find analogous/reusable actions.
- Use risk flags to suppress unsafe candidates.
- Explain why a candidate is shown.
- Collect feedback/correction.

NBA must not:
- skip the user direction choice.
- pretend certainty.
- claim medical, hormonal, legal, financial or social conclusions.
- force one next action.
- use hidden sensitive inference.
- convert category directly to state fact.

10. Examples
============================================================

Example 1:
German grammar practice and English grammar practice are similar because both share language_learning and grammar_practice.
Sleep is relevant to both because sleep may affect focus/recovery, but sleep is not similar to grammar practice.

Example 2:
B2B sales calls and CV preparation may be relevant to career/finance direction.
They are only partially similar if both share business/career context.
Neither implies income received or job success.

Example 3:
Family care activity is relevant to time_pressure, obligation_load and recovery_need.
It is not similar to coding, but it can constrain coding feasibility.

Example 4:
Walking and stretching are similar in physical/recovery context.
Walking may also be relevant to energy/recovery, but it must not be interpreted as automatic muscle growth or medical treatment.

Example 5:
Noise and no-computer context are relevant to environment_fit and focus.
They are not similar to the task itself, but they affect task feasibility.
